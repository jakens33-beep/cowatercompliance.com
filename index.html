System Classifications

We operate all CDPHE-regulated system types

Colorado's water and wastewater systems are classified by how they serve the public. We hold or are pursuing certifications to serve all of them.

Community Water System

CWS — 15+ year-round connections

The most comprehensive regulatory classification. Serves permanent residents year-round. Subject to the full suite of CDPHE drinking water requirements including Lead & Copper Rule, CCR, and disinfection byproduct monitoring.

Mobile Home Parks HOAs Subdivisions Senior Living Small Towns
Non-Transient Non-Community

NTNC — Same 25+ people, 6+ months

Serves the same population regularly but not year-round residential. Subject to significant monitoring requirements including nitrate, Lead & Copper, and bacteriological sampling on a defined schedule.

Schools Workplaces Factories
Transient Non-Community

TNC — Transient population served

Serves a rotating population that is not the same people regularly. Monitoring requirements are less intensive but bacteriological sampling and nitrate monitoring still apply.

RV Parks Campgrounds Rest Stops Summer Camps Churches
Wastewater — NPDES/CDPS Permitted

Wastewater Treatment Facilities

Facilities with a Colorado Discharge Permit System (CDPS) or NPDES permit discharging treated wastewater. Requires Class A Wastewater certified ORC, DMR reporting, and effluent monitoring per permit schedule.

Package Plants Lagoon Systems Industrial WW Small WWTPs
